The Growing Problem in Digital Finance

Modern digital scams don't look like obvious spam anymore. They exploit trust and urgency.

Sophisticated Phishing

Attackers craft convincing impersonation emails pretending to be your bank or shipping courier to harvest login credentials.

Fraudulent Payment Demands

Scammers insist on non-refundable payment methods like Zelle, Venmo, Wire Transfer, or Gift Cards before sending items.

Deceptive Store URLs

Lookalike domains (e.g. paypa1.com or amazon-deals.xyz) fool buyers into submitting credit card details.

Fake E-Commerce Deals

Unrealistic discounts (90% off trending tech) posted on social media storefronts designed solely to take payments without delivering products.
