Digital Threat Defense Guide

Master the key warning signs of modern financial phishing, e-commerce deception, and social engineering traps.

1. Artificial Urgency & Panic

Scammers use high-pressure deadlines ("Account suspended in 1 hour!", "Immediate legal action!") so victims act emotionally without verifying.

Defense Tip: Always pause. Legitimate institutions give reasonable notice and never demand panic action.

2. OTP & 2FA Passcode Safety

One-Time Passcodes (OTP) sent to your mobile phone are master key access tokens to your bank and social accounts.

Defense Tip: Never share an OTP with anyone over the phone or text—not even a customer support agent.

3. Lookalike Web Addresses

Phishing links use visual character tricks (`paypa1.com` instead of `paypal.com` or `apple-id-security.xyz`).

Defense Tip: Inspect the full domain in your address bar and ensure it starts with HTTPS on official company domains.

4. Irreversible Payment Demands

Peer-to-Peer money transfers (Zelle, Venmo, Wire Transfers, Gift Cards, Crypto) carry zero buyer protection if you are defrauded.

Defense Tip: Use credit cards or buyer-protected services (like PayPal Goods & Services) when purchasing from unknown sellers.

5. Unrealistic E-Commerce Deals

High-end electronics or luxury fashion listed for 80% to 90% below market value on social media ads are almost always fake storefronts.

Defense Tip: Research seller domain age and independent reviews before placing orders.

6. What To Do If Targeted

If you accidentally clicked a suspicious link or shared credentials:

  • Change account passwords immediately.
  • Freeze affected bank cards or call your bank directly.
  • Enable 2-Factor Authentication across all accounts.
  • Report the phishing message to official authorities.
